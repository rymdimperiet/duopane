assets/icons.ttf holds a few icons cut from a Nerd Font (https://www.nerdfonts.com,
MIT licence) by packaging/make-icon-font.sh. The icons themselves come from:

- Font Awesome (https://fontawesome.com) - SIL Open Font License 1.1
- Devicons (https://vorillaz.github.io/devicons) - MIT
- Seti UI (https://github.com/jesseweed/seti-ui) - MIT
- Octicons (https://github.com/primer/octicons) - MIT
- Font Logos (https://github.com/lukas-w/font-logos) - Unlicense

The font file was patched from Meslo LG (Apache License 2.0); none of its
letters are included.
